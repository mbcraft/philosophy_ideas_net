Concept for a fruit harvesting wagon for food forests - Version 1.0

Food forests are an interesting idea because the trees within them do not die like those in traditional agriculture (in India, for example, there are mango trees several hundred years old). The problem of harvesting fruit from the highest branches can be solved with a specially designed harvesting wagon. The wagon must have four wheels and a motor for movement. The central part of the wagon must actually be designed as a sort of elevator: using a lever, it must be possible to raise and lower it up to a height of 4-5 meters. The lateral part of this elevator has a ballast on one side and a small balcony on the other that can be extended laterally, also via a special lever. The important thing is that the ballast on the opposite side of the balcony also extends laterally proportionally to balance this lateral balcony. This way, workers have enough space to place baskets and, if necessary, a container in the center of the wagon. For the container, a system with roller tracks, like that of traditional collection wagons, can be used, allowing the container to be unloaded from the rear of the wagon when it is back at ground level. With this system and a sufficiently large wagon, it is also possible to reach heights of more than 4-5 meters, or use stabilizers that can be placed on the ground for greater safety. Furthermore, the wagon could be equipped with solar panels on the upper part for recharging and be completely electric.
